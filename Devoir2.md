## Required Devoir 2 features

Create the service-business prototype with:

- Clinic name and service type
- Services and pricing
- Therapist/team section
- Interactive appointment booking form
- Confirmation screen after booking
- Contact information
- Opening hours
- “Designed by Wissim Elmasry”
- Responsive design
- Polished high-fidelity UI

## Personas

Persona 1:
Busy office worker with back pain who wants to book quickly.

Goal:
Book an appointment as fast as possible.

Persona 2:
Cautious user comparing services and prices before booking.

Goal:
Browse services and prices before deciding.

## Booking form

The form must include:

- service selection
- therapist selection
- date
- time
- name
- email or phone

After submission, show a confirmation screen with the selected details.

## Design

Use the current theme/style of the app as much as possible.

Do not create a completely unrelated visual style.

The Devoir 2 project should match the portfolio’s existing design language while still looking like a professional physiotherapy clinic website.
